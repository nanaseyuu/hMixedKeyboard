# Mixed KB - Privacy Policy (draft for review before publishing)

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Mixed KB is an input method (keyboard) for HarmonyOS. It lets you type
Cantonese and Chinese text using a pinyin-style letter layout, a physical
keyboard, or voice input.

## What the keyboard processes

- Keystrokes you type are processed ON YOUR DEVICE to build a pinyin
  composition and to look up candidate characters in a dictionary that is
  bundled inside the app. Dictionary lookups never leave the device and no
  keystroke content is stored, logged, or transmitted by the keyboard.
- Candidate selections and settings are kept on the device.

## Voice input (optional)

- Voice typing (press-and-hold the space key) uses the on-device speech
  service of HarmonyOS. Your microphone audio is processed by the system
  speech engine to produce text. Mixed KB does not store or transmit your
  audio itself; the system speech service may process audio in the cloud
  as part of HarmonyOS speech recognition. You can simply not use voice
  input, and no microphone audio is then captured.

## Permissions

- Microphone (optional, only when you use voice input).
- Vibrate (key press feedback). No network, location, contacts, storage,
  or identity permissions are requested.

## Data we collect

None. Mixed KB has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no
account system.
